Frequently asked questions

How do I play? Tap a plane whose nose points at a free path. It taxis to a gate, and travelers of the same color board it. When it is full it takes off. Keep gates free for the colors at the front of the line. In the game: Settings > How to play.

I got stuck. Is every level solvable? Yes. Every level is checked by a solver. Use Gate, Sort or Shuffle boosters if you are stuck, or retry.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone? On Android, sign in to Google Play Games (Settings > Cloud save). Your progress is backed up and restored automatically on your new device.

Does the game have ads or purchases? No. Everything is earned by playing.

Can I play offline? Yes. The cloud backup catches up the next time you are online.

How do I delete my data? See the Privacy Policy.

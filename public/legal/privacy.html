Airplane Jam Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Airplane Jam is made by jackale2 ("we"). Short version: the game has no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no accounts of its own, and we do not collect or sell personal information. The only thing that leaves your device is an optional backup of your game progress in your own Google Play Games account.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, unlocked items, daily rewards, missions, achievements, play statistics) and your settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device.

Google Play Games cloud save (Android)

On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a copy of your game progress in Play Games Saved Games, so you can continue on a new phone or after reinstalling. This copy contains only the game progress listed above. It is stored by Google under your Google account and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy; we do not run any server and cannot see it. The game also shows your Play Games display name in Settings. You can play without signing in to Play Games; the game then keeps everything on your device only.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or contacts.
  • No location, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising services.

Permissions

The game uses internet access only for Google Play Games, and vibration for gentle feedback (you can turn vibration off in Settings).

Children

Airplane Jam is a family-friendly puzzle game, but it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • On the device: Settings > Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the app.
  • The cloud copy: open the Google Play Games app, go to your profile settings, and delete the Play Games data for Airplane Jam (or delete your Play Games profile). Reset progress in the game also overwrites the cloud copy.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle personal data (for example optional ads or purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released, and the store listing will say so.

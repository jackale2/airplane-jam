Airplane Jam Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Short version: Airplane Jam does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. The game works fully offline and has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, unlocked items and settings) is saved only on your own device, using the device's local app storage. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or contacts.
  • No location, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services.

Permissions

On phones the game may use vibration for gentle feedback. You can turn it off in Settings. No other permissions are requested.

Children

Airplane Jam is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the app (or clear the site data in your browser). This removes everything the game saved.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle personal data (for example optional ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app store listing will say so.

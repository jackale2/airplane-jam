Airplane Jam Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Short version: Airplane Jam does not collect personal information itself and has no accounts or analytics. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device and usage information to show and measure ads, and to personalize them where allowed. In regions that require it, the game asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change the choice later. See how Google uses data from ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, unlocked items and settings) is saved only on your own device, using the device's local app storage. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or contacts.
  • No location, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • We ourselves collect no advertising identifiers; the ad provider described above may.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services.

Permissions

On phones the game may use vibration for gentle feedback. You can turn it off in Settings. For ads, the app uses an internet connection and the Android advertising ID permission.

Children

Airplane Jam is suitable for all ages. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the app (or clear the site data in your browser). This removes everything the game saved.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle personal data (for example purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app store listing will say so.

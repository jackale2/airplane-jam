# Art direction and feel

Updated Sep 30, 2026. This page records what works in the "jam" puzzle niche and how Airplane Jam matches it.

## What the niche does (research summary)

Traffic, bus and seat "jam" puzzles (Bus Jam, Seat Away, Car Jam, Color Block Jam, and airport variants such as Airport Jam, Plane Sort and Plane Boarding) are among the top grossing hybrid-casual puzzles of 2025. The leaders share a clear playbook:

- **Look.** Bright, saturated pieces on a clean, calm board. Toy-like 2.5D: rounded shapes, glossy highlights, soft drop shadows, top-down camera. Backgrounds are light and low-noise so the colors pop.
- **Readability first.** Every color must be told apart at a glance on a small phone. The best games add shapes or symbols for colorblind players; Bus Jam reviews complain when similar hues are hard to tell apart.
- **Minimal HUD.** Level number in a pill at the top, coins at the top right, boosters as big buttons with count badges at the bottom. Nothing covers the board.
- **Satisfying feedback ("juice").** Passengers hop into vehicles one by one, seats visibly fill, the full vehicle leaves with a whoosh. Squash and stretch on taps, pitch that rises as a streak grows, confetti on a win. Sensory satisfaction is a main retention driver in 2025 puzzle hits.
- **A 10-second hook.** The first level is understood without reading. Tutorials point at the exact piece to tap.
- **Difficulty rhythm.** Easy levels with regular "Hard level" spikes that pay more. Players hate impossible levels and stressful timers.
- **Light meta.** A map or journey, stars or collections, daily rewards and missions, and cosmetics give a reason to come back on day 3 to 7.

## Decisions for Airplane Jam

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Overall look | Bright toy-like 2.5D. Sky gradient, soft pastel scenery per destination, a mid-tone tarmac board so all 8 colors pop, soft shadows under every plane and passenger. |
| Planes | Glossy top-down airliners: cylindrical shading, window rows, colored wings and tail, darker same-hue outline (no black ink). Five liveries as cosmetics. |
| Passengers | Cute chibi travelers: big heads, simple faces, varied hair and skin tones, suitcases and backpacks. The outfit carries the color. Outfit accessories (party hats, beanies, sunglasses, crowns) are cosmetics. |
| Colorblind | Symbols stay on by default: on the plane tail and on each passenger's chest. |
| Gates | Gate stands with seat dots that fill with color as passengers board. |
| Queue | A boarding walkway with rope posts, in loop, star, heart or spiral shapes. The head of the line is marked. |
| HUD | Top: pause, level pill (red "HARD" tag on hard levels), coins. Bottom: three booster buttons with count badges. |
| Motion | Squash on tap, blocker flashes on a bump, planes curve into their gate, passengers hop along an arc, planes bounce as they fill, takeoff with contrail and shadow separation, chain combos ("Nice!", "Great!", "Amazing!"). Reduced-motion setting available. |
| Sound (ASMR) | Calm generative music per destination, boarding pops tuned to a pentatonic scale so every plane plays a rising melody, soft whooshes and chimes, gentle haptics. No harsh buzzers. |
| Difficulty | Every level is solver-checked, no timers. Every 10th level is marked HARD and pays double. |
| Meta | 10 destinations of 20 levels on a scrolling world map, 1 to 3 stars per level, 7-day daily gifts, 3 daily missions, achievements, Zen mode, cosmetics shop. |
| Ads | None at launch. Boosters and continues cost coins, which players earn generously. |
